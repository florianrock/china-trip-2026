🚄 Bullet Train Business Class

Beijing → Shanghai in 4.5h at 350 km/h — lie-flat seats, gourmet meal service, panoramic views. Better than flying in every possible way.

Shanghai Disneyland

The newest and most spectacular Disney park on earth — TRON coaster, world's largest castle, and a fireworks show over the moat that will floor you.

The Bund Waterfront

A mile-long promenade of colonial grandeur facing the futuristic Pudong skyline — Shanghai's most iconic panorama.

Yu Garden & Old City

A 16th-century classical garden of moon gates, koi ponds and dragon walls — plus the original soup dumpling teahouse right on the lotus pond.

Shanghai Tower — Floor 118

The world's 2nd tallest building, 632m up — above the clouds, looking down on the entire city and out to the East China Sea.

The French Concession

Tree-lined Art Deco streets, independent cafes, Xintiandi and Tianzifang — the neighbourhood that makes you want to move to Shanghai.

Zhujiajiao Water Town

A perfectly preserved Ming dynasty canal town 30 km from Shanghai — gondolas, 36 stone bridges and zero skyscrapers.

Huangpu River Night Cruise

The Bund and Pudong from the water at night — both skylines glowing, reflections on the river. The best single view in Shanghai.

Qushui Langting — 24h Luxury Spa

Shanghai's legendary all-night spa resort — thermal pools, massage wing, cinema pods, late-night dim sum and a rooftop hot spring above the city.

🧱 Shanghai LEGOLAND Resort

China's first LEGOLAND — and the world's largest — opened July 2025 in Jinshan. A full outdoor park: 8 themed lands, MINILAND China in millions of bricks, rides and a themed hotel. A full day, made for the kids.

The Forbidden City

Walk through 600 years of imperial history across 180 acres of golden-roofed halls — the world's largest palace complex.

The Great Wall

Stand atop one of humanity's greatest achievements — thousands of kilometres of ancient stone snaking over wild mountain ridges.

Temple of Heaven

A geometric masterpiece of blue rooftiles where emperors prayed for harvests — and where thousands of locals do tai chi at dawn every single morning.

Summer Palace

290 hectares of imperial lakes, willow-lined corridors and marble bridges — Beijing's most romantic escape, missed by most tourists.

Hutong Neighbourhood Tour

Wander Beijing's ancient alleyways before they disappear — rickshaws, jianbing street crepes, courtyard homes and rooftop views of the Drum Tower.

Giant Panda Baby Zone

Meet the world's most adorable cubs at the Chengdu Research Base — up close with baby pandas in their bamboo paradise.

Nanxiang Xiaolongbao

The original 1900 soup dumpling restaurant inside Yu Garden — 18 hand-pleated folds, scalding broth inside. The benchmark every other dumpling is judged against.

Shanghainese Classics

Hong shao rou braised pork belly, hairy crab in peak October season, drunken chicken — the richest, sweetest cuisine in China at Jesse Restaurant.

Shanghai Noodles & Street Food

Scallion oil noodles since 1932, shengjian pan-fried buns, wonton soup — Shanghai's essential street-food crawl for under CHF 5.

大壶春 — $3 Michelin Pan-fried Buns

Shanghai's most iconic shengjian bao since 1932 — golden crackling bottoms, soup inside, straight from cast-iron skillets. Michelin Bib Gourmand for under $3. Sichuan Middle Road.

Peking Duck at Da Dong

The dish Beijing invented, perfected over 600 years — crackling lacquered skin, tableside carving, gossamer pancakes. The only Peking Duck that matters.

Sijimingfu — Imperial Duck

A Qing dynasty courtyard steps from the Forbidden City, four generations of family recipe — hickory smoke ovens, 108-slice carving ceremony.

满恒记 — Instant Boiled Mutton

Beijing's legendary copper hot pot institution — paper-thin Inner Mongolian lamb, legendary sesame dipping sauce, honest old-school Beijing hospitality.

很久以前 — Mutton Skewers

Beijing's most obsessive lamb skewer chain — Hulunbuir grassland lamb on a rotating smokeless grill, 5 years on Dianping's Must-Eat List. The Haidilao of BBQ.

Authentic Sichuan Hotpot

Dive into Chengdu's soul food — a bubbling cauldron of numbing spices, fresh meats and vegetables, best shared with everyone.

Mapo Tofu at Chen's

The original recipe since 1862 at the restaurant that invented it — silken tofu in scorching mala sauce, eaten in the city that gave this dish to the world.

锦城印象火锅 — Wagyu Hotpot

Michelin Selected wagyu hotpot in Wuhou — Australian M9+ wagyu shoulder cooked tableside in beef-tallow Sichuan broth, 5 minutes from the Wuhou Temple.
