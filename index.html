24-Hour Spa Experience

Melt away in one of Shanghai's legendary luxury spas — thermal pools, herbal treatments, and city views that never sleep.

The Bund Waterfront

A mile-long promenade of colonial grandeur facing the futuristic Pudong skyline — Shanghai's most iconic panorama.

Giant Panda Baby Zone

Meet the world's most adorable cubs at the Chengdu Research Base — up close with baby pandas in their bamboo paradise.

Authentic Sichuan Hotpot

Dive into Chengdu's soul food — a bubbling cauldron of numbing spices, fresh meats and vegetables, best shared with everyone.

The Forbidden City

Walk through 600 years of imperial history across 180 acres of golden-roofed halls — the world's largest palace complex.

The Great Wall

Stand atop one of humanity's greatest achievements — thousands of kilometres of ancient stone snaking over wild mountain ridges.

Peking Duck at Da Dong

The dish Beijing invented, perfected over 600 years — crackling lacquered skin, tableside carving, gossamer pancakes. The only Peking Duck that matters.

Temple of Heaven

A geometric masterpiece of blue rooftiles where emperors prayed for harvests — and where thousands of locals do tai chi at dawn every single morning.

Summer Palace

290 hectares of imperial lakes, willow-lined corridors and marble bridges — Beijing's most romantic escape, missed by most tourists.

Sijimingfu — Imperial Duck

A Qing dynasty courtyard steps from the Forbidden City, four generations of family recipe — Beijing's most iconic Peking Duck with hickory smoke and 108-slice tableside carving.

Hutong Neighbourhood Tour

Wander Beijing's ancient alleyways before they disappear — rickshaws, jianbing street crepes, courtyard homes and rooftop views of the Drum Tower.
